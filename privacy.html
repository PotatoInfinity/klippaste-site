7. Retention and deletion

  • License records (key, hwid_hash, dev_pub, activations): kept for the life of the license plus a fraud and audit period required for tax, accounting, and abuse prevention.
  • Feedback and support tickets: kept until your request is resolved, plus a reasonable follow-up period for quality and dispute handling.
  • Infrastructure and security logs: kept short-term (days to weeks) and then deleted or aggregated, unless needed longer to investigate abuse or incidents.
  • Future order records: kept as required by tax and accounting law.

Deletion requests are honored subject to exceptions: we retain data where needed for audit, fraud prevention, legal claims or obligations, and in backups that roll over on their normal cycle. Backups are not rewritten for individual deletions but age out.

10. Security and breach notice

We use transport encryption, least-access controls, and pseudonymous device identifiers (hwid_hash, dev_pub) instead of raw hardware identifiers. No method is fully secure, but we work to protect license, support, and log data.

In case of a personal-data breach, we will assess the risk, notify the competent supervisory authority without undue delay where required, and notify affected individuals where the risk requires it, describing the nature, likely consequences, and measures taken.

11. Data sources (Art. 14)
