Goodwill

60-day discretionary goodwill credit

Not a guarantee. A goodwill credit we may grant when the apps genuinely fail for you.

  • Ask within 60 days of purchase with your order id and checkout email.
  • One per order. Abuse voids eligibility for that order and any later goodwill.
  • Granted credits revoke the license keys from that order; replacements are never issued on the same order.
  • Paid back to the original payment method by our Merchant of Record, Lemon Squeezy.

30 days automatic vs 60 days on request

  • 30 days post-order is automatic only: referral credits qualify automatically once the refund window has elapsed, and refunded orders claw back via the order_refunded event. Nothing is paid back to you automatically at 30 days.
  • 60 days is on request only: the goodwill credit above never happens on its own — you must contact us within 60 days and we decide case by case.

EU customers: express waiver at checkout

  • Keys are delivered immediately as digital content. EU withdrawal rights end when supply begins with your express consent plus your acknowledgement that you lose the right once supply starts.
  • Seller and Merchant of Record is Lemon Squeezy; checkout states this before you pay.
  • Checkout carries an unchecked checkbox you must tick: you give express consent to immediate supply and acknowledge the loss of withdrawal.
  • A policy page alone is insufficient to create this waiver — only the express consent plus acknowledgement checkbox at checkout creates it.

Payment disputes: contact us first

  • Contact us first with your order id so we can check the license and the charge before you file.
  • While a dispute is open the license seats from that order stay locked pending the outcome.
  • Granted or dispute-decided credits go back via the original payment method through Lemon Squeezy.
